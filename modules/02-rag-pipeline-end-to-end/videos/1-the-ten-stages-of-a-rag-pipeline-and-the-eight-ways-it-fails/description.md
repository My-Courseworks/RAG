Module two of a self-study course on retrieval-augmented generation. We follow one logistics question, the dangerous-goods surcharge for lithium batteries on the Rotterdam to Singapore lane, through all ten stages of a RAG pipeline and watch it come back with a confident, cited, wrong answer. Along the way: the ingest and query paths, how each stage maps to a LlamaIndex object, the F1 to F8 failure taxonomy and how to triage with it, what to retain so evaluation is possible, and which three stages to instrument first.
